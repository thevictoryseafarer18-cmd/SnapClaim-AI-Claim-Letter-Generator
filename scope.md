# SnapClaim: Scope
**In scope:** photo upload (1–5, 5MB each, client-side compression) → AI damage analysis → damage report card → AI claim letter → editable preview → PDF export.
**Out of scope:** accounts, database, insurer integrations, payments, email.
**Success:** a dented-bumper photo yields a coherent, terminology-correct letter in under 30 seconds, editable and exportable as PDF.
